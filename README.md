## Understanding Customer support chatbot
<bold>what is and what does customer support chatbot do ?</bold>
Customer support chatbot automate routine inquiries, offering 24/7 assistance and reduce operational costs. Key use cases are:
- 24/7 answer FAQs
- Order Tracking and management
- Technical support and Troubleshooting
- Booking and Scheduling
- Returns and Refunds
- Personalization recommendation

  
Common Customer Support Chatbot Failures
- Failed Intent Recognition
- Poor Escalation Pathways
- Lack of Context and Personalization
- Lack of Empathy
